How to Read This Page (Quick Guide)

Why is the power zero at night?

Solar photovoltaic cells require photons of light from the sun to knock electrons free and generate an electric current. At night, with no sunlight hitting the panels, generation naturally drops to zero Watts while the panels rest.

What is the difference between kW and kWh?

Think of kW (kilowatt) like speedometer speed (km/h) — it tells you how fast electricity is being produced right this second. Think of kWh (kilowatt-hour) like total distance travelled (km) — it is the total volume of energy gathered over time. 1 kW running for 1 hour makes 1 kWh (or 1 unit on your electricity bill).

How are faults and problems detected?

Our system compares live power against a real-time physics model calculated from satellite solar irradiance and ambient temperature. An alert is flagged if inverter output drops by more than 50% below expected for 3 readings in a row while sunlight is at least 150 W/m².

Where do the numbers on this page come from?

Live electrical readings are logged automatically by the inverter telemetry device into Google Sheets. Sunshine irradiance and weather conditions are sourced from Open-Meteo satellite atmospheric forecasts specifically for Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh.

How are financial savings and CO₂ estimated?

Financial savings are calculated using the college institutional power tariff of ₹7 per unit (kWh). Carbon reduction is estimated using India's average electrical grid factor of 0.7 kg of carbon dioxide avoided for every kWh of clean solar electricity harvested.
